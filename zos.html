Optical system design

Laser-to-fibre coupling and three classic imaging systems, designed and analysed in Zemax OpticStudio. The imaging systems are evaluated at the visible F, d and C lines (0.486, 0.588 and 0.656 µm). Every number below is taken from the OpticStudio analyses and reports linked under each design.

Laser-to-fibre coupling: single-mode vs multimode

A collimated 632.8 nm Gaussian beam (waist radius 1.15 mm) focused by one stock lens, a Thorlabs C220TMD-A moulded asphere (f = 11.00 mm), into a single-mode fibre (MFD 4.0 µm) and into 50 µm and 105 µm core multimode fibres (NA 0.22). The focused spot (1.96 µm radius) is matched to the single-mode fibre mode. Coupling was computed with OpticStudio's Huygens fibre-coupling analysis and Physical Optics Propagation, and an alignment sweep (decenter, focus, tilt; 567 POP runs) was scripted in Python through the ZOS-API.

Results
SMF coupling efficiency98.8% (−0.05 dB); 3 methods agree within ~0.1%
Focused spot / fibre mode1.956 µm / 2.0 µm radius (99.95% overlap)
SMF lateral offset, −1 dB±0.94 µm
SMF focus offset, −1 dB+19 / −20 µm
SMF fibre tilt, −1 dB2.8°
MMF coupling (bare end face)95.4% (50 and 105 µm core)
MMF lateral offset, −1 dB±24 µm (50 µm) · ±52 µm (105 µm)

Peak efficiency is the same for all three fibres; the multimode cores relax the lateral tolerance by about 26× and 55×, at the cost of a multimode output.

Achromatic doublet

A two-element achromat, which pairs a crown and a flint glass to bring two wavelengths to a common focus. Analysed on axis and at 1° field.

Performance
Wavelengths0.486 / 0.588 / 0.656 µm
Field angles0°, 1°
Image height at 1°3.055 mm
RMS spot radius2.542 µm (0°) · 2.652 µm (1°)
GEO spot radius6.176 µm (0°) · 4.741 µm (1°)
Max. chromatic focal shift87.06 µm
Diffraction-limited range115.22 µm
Total axial length178.75 mm

The chromatic focal shift across 0.486–0.656 µm (87.06 µm) stays inside the diffraction-limited range (115.22 µm).

Wollaston landscape lens

An early photographic lens: a single meniscus element with an aperture stop, giving a wide field of view with better off-axis sharpness than a simple lens. Analysed at four field angles up to 22.5°.

Modified Ritchey–Chrétien telescope

A reflecting telescope with hyperbolic primary and secondary mirrors, which cancel spherical aberration and coma, plus a refractive element near the focal plane. The spots stay well inside the Airy disc across the field.

Performance
Wavelengths0.486 / 0.588 / 0.656 µm
Field angles0°, 0.175°, 0.35°
Image height0 / 11.489 / 23.067 mm
RMS spot radius0.629 / 0.752 / 0.587 µm
GEO spot radius0.981 / 1.902 / 1.101 µm
Airy radius7.693 µm
Total axial length880.17 mm

Values are listed in field order 0°, 0.175°, 0.35°. The largest RMS spot (0.752 µm) is about a tenth of the Airy radius, so the design is diffraction-limited over the full field.
